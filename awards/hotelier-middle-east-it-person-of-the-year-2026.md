## 3. INTELLIGENT SERVICE ROBOT PROGRAMME

### How it was built
Rather than treating autonomous service as a technology demonstration, the IT Manager led a structured commercial, technical and operational evaluation before any commitment was made. This included a full review of the vendor contract, a formal concerns register covering liability, service levels, data handling and exit terms, and a handover of findings to Finance for sign off. Only once that evaluation cleared did a 25 day live operational pilot proceed, with every delivery, timing and route logged automatically through the device performance dashboard rather than by manual tracking. Every operational, financial and guest experience metric was then compiled into a formal business case, which has since been presented to and adopted by InterContinental Abu Dhabi, with a second IHG property progressing toward implementation.

### Operational impact

Demand rose steadily through the pilot to a peak of **94 deliveries in a single day**. ### Guest experience impact
Guest satisfaction increased by **10%** during the pilot, evidenced by Medallia survey results and positive online guest reviews. Beyond faster delivery, guests actively engaged with the robot as part of their stay, creating memorable moments while releasing colleagues to spend more time on personalised service.

## 6. STEWARDING APP: DIGITISING BACK OF HOUSE OPERATIONS

### How it was built
Stewarding is the highest cost, lowest visibility function in most hotel back of house operations. Breakage, chemical consumption, equipment location, casual labour deployment and HACCP compliance evidence were all tracked on paper, which meant losses were discovered at inventory count rather than at the point they occurred, and compliance evidence was reconstructed rather than recorded.

The IT Manager designed and built the Stewarding App in house to close that gap. The application digitises the full stewarding cycle: task assignment and completion by shift and area, breakage and loss capture at the point of incident with item, quantity and cost, chemical issue and consumption tracking against par levels, equipment and operating equipment movement between outlets, banqueting and stores, and time stamped cleaning and HACCP compliance logs available for audit on demand. All data feeds the same Business Intelligence layer as the revenue and F&B platforms, so the Executive Chef, Director of Operations and Finance see stewarding cost in the same daily view as revenue.
